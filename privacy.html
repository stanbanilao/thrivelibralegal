Privacy information

Libra Credit is a service of Libra Legal SA. This page explains the privacy principles used on this website. A final operational privacy notice should be updated to reflect the exact systems, service providers and data-processing arrangements used for the live assessment process.

Information you choose to provide

When you continue to an assessment or contact process, you may be asked for personal information needed to understand or support your credit-profile enquiry. Only provide information through the secure process provided for that purpose.

Credit and identity information

Credit-profile services can involve sensitive personal and financial information. Access to credit-bureau information should only take place with the required authority or consent and through the relevant lawful process.

Service providers

Libra Credit may use approved third-party systems or service providers to support assessments, credit-profile retrieval, communication or case processing. Where this occurs, the applicable operational privacy notice and consent wording should explain the relevant processing.

Your choices

You may choose not to continue with an assessment. Where applicable, you may also request access to or correction of personal information held about you, subject to legal and operational requirements.

Important

This page is an initial website privacy notice and should not be treated as a substitute for a complete POPIA compliance framework, information-officer process or service-specific consent wording.
